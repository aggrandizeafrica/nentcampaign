Support for every stage of communication

Our speech pathology team diagnoses and treats communication and swallowing difficulties in people of all ages.

Children

Speech delay, articulation, language and social communication support for little ones and school-age children.

Adults

Speech and language therapy after stroke, injury or illness, and support for stammering and clarity.

Voice therapy

For hoarseness, vocal strain and professional voice users such as teachers, singers and speakers.

Feeding & swallowing

Assessment and therapy for feeding and swallowing difficulties in children and adults.
